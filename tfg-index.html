01

Enterprise Knowledge Base Migration

Salesforce Knowledge · CSV imports · Information governance

Context

The client was moving content from two legacy intranet environments into Salesforce Knowledge. The source environments had fragmented information structures, inconsistent classification, duplicated and outdated material, and different content formats. The work required both a migration process and a sustainable governance approach for the destination knowledge base.

Role and contribution

  • Performed responsibilities spanning operational project coordination, information architecture, editorial leadership and hands-on content production.
  • Reviewed legacy content to support decisions to migrate, revise, consolidate or retire material.
  • Identified overlapping and duplicate articles; reviewed cross-links and supported consolidation.
  • Developed and maintained classification principles, controlled vocabulary, synonyms, definitions and semantic relationships.
  • Created, edited, classified, reviewed and published content in Salesforce Knowledge, including CSV imports.
  • Maintained Excel migration controls covering source/destination references, titles, owners, deadlines, stages, classifications, blockers and Salesforce article IDs.
  • Created governance documentation, editorial guidelines and checklists; trained writers and users.

Systems and controls

Salesforce Knowledge, CSV imports into Salesforce, Excel, Thesa and Tematres. Security controls experienced: VPN access, confidentiality agreement, individual credentials and user-specific access profiles. I followed these controls as a user; I did not design or administer the client's security architecture.

01b

Taxonomy & Controlled Vocabulary Workstream

A deeper view of the classification and vocabulary work within Case 01

Controlled vocabulary structure

The classification system used three dimensions: Customer Profile, Product/Service, and Topic/Subject. Topic/Subject was assigned a single primary subject based on relevance; Profile and Product could admit multiple classifications depending on content. The controlled vocabulary included preferred terms, variants/synonyms, definitions, hierarchical and associative relationships, usage criteria and validation with subject-matter experts.

Scope and limits

I created the initial base of more than 700 terms from a starting tool that contained approximately 150–200 terms. The later expansion of the vocabulary to 2,700+ was a broader company/committee initiative; I did not individually author all 2,700 terms. This demonstration illustrates the logic of classification and term structure, not a reproduction of actual terms or rules.

Case Study 02

Census Field Operations and Data Management

Field Enumerator Supervisor · Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) · Direct institutional engagement following a public selection process

02

Census Field Operations

IBGE · User provisioning · Device data transfer · Access controls

Context

The role supported census field operations in which enumerators used handheld devices to collect information in the field. I was responsible for supporting the field team and the operational flow through which information collected on devices was transferred into the central system.

Role and contribution

  • Supervised field enumerators and supported the day-to-day operation of data collection.
  • Registered enumerators as users in the organization's proprietary data-collection system.
  • Assigned user permissions and access profiles according to operational responsibilities.
  • Collected handheld devices and coordinated the transfer/import of field-collected data into the central system.
  • Worked under mandatory institutional training and with individual credentials and user-specific access profiles.

Security and access

The confirmed controls for this role were mandatory institutional training, individual user accounts/credentials and user-specific access profiles/permissions. No additional controls are claimed here.
